Chatterbox TTS Server needs more memory

This service has less memory or disk than the Chatterbox model needs, so the model was not loaded.

The Chatterbox-Turbo model runs on CPU in full precision and needs at least 6 GB of RAM (8 GB recommended) and about 3 GB of volume space for its model files. That means a Railway Hobby plan or higher: the Free and Trial plans allow 0.5 to 1 GB per service.

Raise the service's memory limit (service Settings, Resource limits) to 6 GB or more, then redeploy. The model downloads once into the volume and loads automatically. Your password and API key stay the same.
